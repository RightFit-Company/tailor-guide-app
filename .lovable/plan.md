# Mobile layout optimisation

## What will change
- Simplify the top navigation on phones so the brand and main actions fit without crowding.
- Reduce oversized spacing, headings, shadows, and card padding on narrow screens while preserving the Playful Tailor style.
- Make measurement controls, step indicators, and primary actions easier to scan and tap one-handed.
- Tighten the wardrobe catalog and outfit controls so cards, labels, and buttons stay readable without horizontal overflow.
- Keep tablet and desktop layouts unchanged where possible.

## Verification
- Check the main fit flow and wardrobe at a phone-sized viewport.
- Confirm there is no horizontal scrolling, clipped text, overlapping controls, or broken popup content.
- Confirm the preview still builds successfully.
